Workspace Assistant – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Who uses this app

Workspace Assistant is used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the developer's consent, the app accesses the developer's own Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Sheets, Docs, Tasks, Contacts (read-only) and YouTube (read-only) data through the Google OAuth scopes the developer granted.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out tasks the developer requests. OAuth tokens and retrieved data are kept on the developer's own computer. The app has no server and does not collect data elsewhere. Data may be processed by AI assistant services that the developer chooses to use for these tasks.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties except as described above. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
